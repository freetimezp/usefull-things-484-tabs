BREAKING NEWS • ISSUE NO. 0484

WANTED DEAD OR ALIVE

The notorious outlaw returns to the Dakota territory. Sheriff warns residents to stay alert.

BY JONATHAN WEST 5 MIN READ
SPECIAL REPORT WANTED THE OUTLAW $5,000 REWARD

A quiet morning in the Dakota territory was interrupted yesterday when reports emerged of a notorious outlaw spotted near Dusty Creek.

Local residents reported seeing a lone rider approaching the settlement shortly before sunset. Witnesses described a mysterious figure wearing a dark coat and a wide-brimmed hat, traveling on a powerful black horse.

The sheriff's office has confirmed that the individual matches the description of a wanted criminal believed to be responsible for several robberies across the region.

THE SHERIFF'S WARNING

Authorities are asking citizens to remain cautious and report any suspicious activity. Residents are strongly advised not to approach the suspect.

"We intend to bring this individual to justice. Anyone with information should contact the sheriff's office." — SHERIFF WILLIAM CARTER

The reward has now been increased to $5,000. Further information will be published as the investigation develops.

— END OF REPORT — THE SOUTH DAKOTA GAZETTE
